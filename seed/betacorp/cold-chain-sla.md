# BetaCorp Cold-Chain Monitoring SLA

BetaCorp Logistics monitors refrigerated trailers (reefers) for customers moving produce, dairy and pharmaceuticals.

## Temperature excursions

An excursion is any period where cargo temperature leaves the customer's configured band. Excursions under 15 minutes are logged; excursions over 15 minutes trigger an alert to the account manager; excursions over 60 minutes require a written incident report within 24 hours.

For GDP-regulated pharmaceutical freight, any excursion over 15 minutes requires a deviation report to the customer's quality team.

Recommended steps after an excursion alert:

1. Confirm the reefer unit setpoint and door-open events in the telematics portal.
2. Contact the driver to verify cargo condition and unit status.
3. Notify the customer's account manager with timestamps and peak temperature.
4. File the incident or deviation report if the duration thresholds are met.

## Service tiers

Platinum and Gold customers receive 24x7 alert response. Silver customers receive business-hours response.
