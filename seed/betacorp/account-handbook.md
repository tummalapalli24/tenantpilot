# BetaCorp Account Team Handbook

## At-risk accounts

An account is marked at_risk when it has three or more open service cases or a renewal within 60 days with unresolved complaints. At-risk accounts get a weekly check-in from their account manager and a service review before renewal.

Recommended steps for an at-risk account:

1. Review open service cases with the operations lead.
2. Schedule a service review call with the customer before the renewal date.
3. Offer a corrective action plan with dated commitments.

## Escalation contacts

Operational incidents go to the BetaCorp operations desk. Contract and renewal questions go to the named account manager. TenantPilot does not create tickets for BetaCorp; the assistant should tell the user whom to contact.
