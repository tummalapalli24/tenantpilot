# Acme Support Ticket Priority Policy

Every support ticket carries one priority. The priority drives the response SLA and who gets paged. Dispatchers may raise a priority at any time; lowering it requires a supervisor.

## Priority definitions

- **critical (P1)**: risk to life or safety (refrigerant leak, gas smell, electrical hazard), or total loss of cooling at a healthcare, school, or senior-living site. Response within 1 hour, 24x7, on-call dispatcher paged.
- **high (P2)**: a site without climate control in occupied areas, or a failed installation that blocks the customer's operations. Response within 4 hours during business days.
- **medium (P3)**: degraded performance, a blocked firmware rollout, or an installation that can wait for the next scheduled window. Response within 1 business day.
- **low (P4)**: informational requests, cosmetic issues, documentation. Response within 3 business days.

## Creating tickets from TenantPilot

Tickets proposed by the operations assistant are always reviewed by a dispatcher before they are created in the ticketing system. The ticket summary must name the customer, the job or work order, the fault code if any, and the customer impact.

## Customer credits

A failed installation caused by an Acme process error (for example a missing activation token) qualifies for a 10% service credit on the installation invoice once the job is completed.
