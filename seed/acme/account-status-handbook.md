# Acme Account Status Handbook

## Account states

- **ACTIVE**: normal service. Work may be scheduled.
- **SUSPENDED**: billing hold. No new work orders or tickets may be scheduled until Finance clears the hold. Emergency safety calls are still answered, and Finance is notified.
- **CLOSED**: contract ended. Refer requests to the Sales team.

## Service plans

- **Standard Care**: business-hours support, quarterly maintenance.
- **Pro Service**: extended hours, priority scheduling, free re-visits for Acme-caused failures.
- **Enterprise 24x7**: round-the-clock response and a named account manager.

## Communicating delays

When a job slips, contact the customer's primary contact the same day, state the cause in plain language, give a realistic restore time, and confirm in writing.
