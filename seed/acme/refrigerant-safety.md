# Acme Refrigerant and Compressor Safety Runbook

## E-CMP-503: Compressor high-pressure alarm

The compressor tripped on high discharge pressure. Combined with oily residue near service valves or a hissing sound, it indicates a probable refrigerant leak. Treat it as a safety incident until a certified technician rules a leak out.

Resolution steps:

1. Shut down the affected unit at the disconnect and keep it locked out.
2. Ventilate the mechanical room and keep occupants away from the unit.
3. Open a critical (P1) ticket and page the on-call dispatcher.
4. Dispatch an EPA Section 608 certified technician to locate and repair the leak.
5. Document recovered refrigerant quantity for EPA reporting.

## Guest-occupied and vulnerable sites

For hotels, hospitals, schools and senior living, arrange temporary cooling within 2 hours if the repair will take longer than 4 hours.
